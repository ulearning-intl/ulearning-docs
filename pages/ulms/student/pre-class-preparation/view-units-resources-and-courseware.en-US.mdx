# View modules, resources, and courseware

## 1. Navigation path

**[Course details]–[Modules/Courseware/Resources]**

![Open course modules, courseware, and resources](/En/Images-New/uLMS/view-units-resources-and-courseware/1.png)

## 2. Steps

### Study according to the syllabus through modules

After entering a course, you can view the overall teaching plan in the Modules section and quickly open the learning activities you need to complete.

Modules may not contain all course content. You can view content that has not been added to a module through the Announcements, Courseware, Assignments, and other menus on the left side of the course.

![View learning activities through course modules](/En/Images-New/uLMS/view-units-resources-and-courseware/2.png)

### Study courseware

Each course may have multiple sets of courseware. Click the courseware name at the top to switch between them.

Select the module you want to study, then click **[Start learning/Continue learning]** to open the course learning page. Course content can include videos, text, exercises, and other content types.

![Select courseware and start learning](/En/Images-New/uLMS/view-units-resources-and-courseware/3.png)

On the course player page, use the table of contents on the left to switch between course sections. Click **[Next page]** or **[Previous page]** below to switch pages. After completing a module, click **[Return to courseware]** to return to the course module page. Click **[Collapse table of contents]** to hide the table of contents on the left.

![Use the courseware player](/En/Images-New/uLMS/view-units-resources-and-courseware/4.png)

If your learning is interrupted, the system displays a prompt. Click **[Continue learning]** to continue.

![Continue studying courseware after an interruption](/En/Images-New/uLMS/view-units-resources-and-courseware/5.png)

### Study course resources

Teachers publish course-related materials in the Resources section for students to study and download. A red dot and a red button indicate that a resource has not been viewed.

![View course resources](/En/Images-New/uLMS/view-units-resources-and-courseware/6.png)

To download a resource, click **[Download]** in the upper-right corner of the page.

![Download a course resource](/En/Images-New/uLMS/view-units-resources-and-courseware/7.png)
