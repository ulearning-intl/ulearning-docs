# Reset or change password

## 1. Navigation path

Path 1: **[Not logged in]–[Forgot password]**

![Open Forgot password before logging in](/En/Images-New/uLMS/student-reset-or-change-password/1.png)

Path 2: **[Logged in]–[Personal information]–[Account management]–[Change password]**

![Open Change password after logging in](/En/Images-New/uLMS/student-reset-or-change-password/2.png)

## 2. Steps

### Reset your password

If you have forgotten your password, you can reset it by email.

**Method 1: Reset it yourself**

![Open the Forgot password page](/En/Images-New/uLMS/student-reset-or-change-password/3.png)

![Reset your password by email](/En/Images-New/uLMS/student-reset-or-change-password/4.png)

**Method 2: Ask your course teacher to reset it**

The teacher can reset a student's password through **[Members]–[Students]**.

![Teacher resets a student's password](/En/Images-New/uLMS/student-reset-or-change-password/5.png)

**Method 3: Contact platform customer support to change it**

### Change your password

Use this option to change your current password after logging in. Enter your current password and new password, confirm the new password, and then change it.

![Enter the current and new passwords to change the password](/En/Images-New/uLMS/student-reset-or-change-password/6.png)
