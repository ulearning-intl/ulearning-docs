# Complete assignments

## 1. Navigation path

**[Course details]–[Assignments]**

![Open course assignments](/En/Images-New/uLMS/complete-assignments/1.png)

## 2. Steps

Assignments are divided into three types: individual assignments, group assignments, and quizzes. Complete them within the specified submission period. If an assignment is not submitted by the deadline, it can only be viewed.

### Individual assignments

#### 1. View individual-assignment requirements

Click **[Do assignment]** in the list to open the assignment details. You can view the assignment content, settings, completion period, and other information.

![View individual-assignment requirements](/En/Images-New/uLMS/complete-assignments/2.png)

#### 2. Complete an individual assignment

You can complete an assignment using text, tables, equations, attachments, audio recordings, and other content. Save unfinished work as a draft or submit it after completion.

![Complete and submit an individual assignment](/En/Images-New/uLMS/complete-assignments/3.png)

#### 3. Withdraw an individual-assignment submission

Students can withdraw and resubmit an assignment before it is graded. It cannot be withdrawn after grading. Click **[Withdraw and resubmit]** to perform this action.

![Withdraw and resubmit an individual assignment](/En/Images-New/uLMS/complete-assignments/4.png)

#### 4. Revise an assignment returned by the teacher

After grading, the teacher returns an assignment if it does not meet the requirements. Students can view the assignment status in the list. If it displays "Revision required," complete and submit the assignment again.

![View an individual assignment requiring revision](/En/Images-New/uLMS/complete-assignments/5.png)

![Revise and resubmit a returned individual assignment](/En/Images-New/uLMS/complete-assignments/6.png)

#### 5. View shared assignments

The teacher can share outstanding assignments with students. Students can view them under **[Shared assignments]** in the assignment details. When an individual assignment is shared, a "Shared" label appears after its grade.

![View shared assignments](/En/Images-New/uLMS/complete-assignments/7.png)

### Group assignments

Only one student in a group needs to submit the group assignment.

#### 1. Not assigned to a group

Students must join a group before they can complete a group assignment. If a student has not been assigned to a group, the system displays a corresponding prompt. Contact the course teacher in this situation.

![View the not-assigned-to-a-group prompt](/En/Images-New/uLMS/complete-assignments/8.png)

#### 2. Complete a group assignment

You can complete an assignment using text, tables, equations, attachments, audio recordings, and other content. Save unfinished work as a draft or submit it after completion.

For a group assignment, students can view group members, post assignment discussions, and browse discussions from other groups. Only one student in each group needs to submit the assignment. Communicate with the other group members and confirm the submission before submitting it.

![Complete and submit a group assignment](/En/Images-New/uLMS/complete-assignments/9.png)

When viewing discussions from other groups, use the left side of the page to switch groups.

![Switch groups and view other group discussions](/En/Images-New/uLMS/complete-assignments/10.png)

#### 3. Withdraw a group-assignment submission

Students can withdraw and resubmit an assignment before it is graded. It cannot be withdrawn after grading. Click **[Withdraw and resubmit]** to perform this action.

![Withdraw and resubmit a group assignment](/En/Images-New/uLMS/complete-assignments/11.png)

#### 4. Revise an assignment returned by the teacher

After grading, the teacher returns an assignment if it does not meet the requirements. Students can view the assignment status in the list. If it displays "Revision required," complete and submit the assignment again.

![View a group assignment requiring revision](/En/Images-New/uLMS/complete-assignments/12.png)

#### 5. Group-assignment peer-review

If the teacher sets a group assignment to "Group peer-review," each group must score other groups' assignments after the assignment deadline and before the peer-review deadline.

![View group-assignment peer-review tasks](/En/Images-New/uLMS/complete-assignments/13.png)

Click **[Review tasks]** to score other groups' assignments and members of your own group.

![Complete group and group-member peer-review](/En/Images-New/uLMS/complete-assignments/14.png)

### Quizzes

#### 1. View quiz requirements

Click **[Do assignment]** in the list to open the quiz details. You can view the quiz's basic information, available period, requirements, and other information. Click **[Start answering]** to open the quiz page.

![View quiz requirements and start answering](/En/Images-New/uLMS/complete-assignments/15.png)

#### 2. Complete a quiz

After completing the quiz, click **[Submit]**. The teacher sets the number of allowed submissions when publishing the quiz. Check the allowed number carefully when submitting.

![Complete and submit a quiz](/En/Images-New/uLMS/complete-assignments/16.png)

After submission, the system automatically displays the quiz score.

![View the quiz score](/En/Images-New/uLMS/complete-assignments/17.png)

#### 3. View response records

Click **[View records]** to open the response-record details. Use the submission times at the top to switch between different submissions.

![Open quiz response records](/En/Images-New/uLMS/complete-assignments/18.png)

![Switch between different quiz submissions](/En/Images-New/uLMS/complete-assignments/19.png)
