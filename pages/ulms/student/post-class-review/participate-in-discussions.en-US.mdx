# Participate in discussions

## 1. Navigation path

**[Course details]–[Discussions]**

![Open course discussions](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-discussions/1.png)

## 2. Steps

### View the discussion list

Students can view course-related discussions in the course discussion list and click **[View]** to open the details and participate.

![View the course discussion list](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-discussions/2.png)

### Participate in a discussion

#### 1. Post a response

Students can enter a response under a discussion published by the teacher. Responses can include text, images, attachments, emojis, and other content. Click **[Submit]** when finished.

![Post a response in a discussion](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-discussions/3.png)

#### 2. Comment on a response

Click **[Comments]** to expand a text box and enter a comment. You can also like a response.

![Comment on or like a discussion response](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-discussions/4.png)

#### 3. Manage your responses

You can reply to, like, edit, or delete your own responses. Deletion cannot be undone.

![Manage your discussion responses](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-discussions/5.png)

### View the word cloud

Use **[Word cloud]** to view frequently used keywords in the discussion.

![Open the discussion word cloud](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-discussions/6.png)

![View frequently used discussion keywords](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-discussions/7.png)

### View point rules and earn points

In the upper-right corner of each discussion, students can view the points they have earned and click **[Point rules]** to view the detailed rules.

![View discussion points and point rules](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-discussions/8.png)
