# Take exams

## 1. Navigation path

**[Course details]–[Exams]**

![Open course exams](/En/Images-New/uLMS/take-exams/1.png)

## 2. Steps

### Enter an exam

Click **[Exams]** to view all exams arranged by the teacher, including their available periods, allowed attempts, and scores. Click **[Start exam]** to take an exam.

![View the exam list and start an exam](/En/Images-New/uLMS/take-exams/2.png)

### Start an exam

After entering an exam, you can view its details, including the enabled proctoring methods. Click **[Start exam]** to open the exam page.

If facial recognition is enabled for the exam, the student must turn on the camera and pass facial verification before opening the exam page.

![View exam details and proctoring methods](/En/Images-New/uLMS/take-exams/3.png)

After passing facial verification, click **[Start exam]** to open the exam page.

![Start the exam after passing facial verification](/En/Images-New/uLMS/take-exams/4.png)

The exam page is shown below.

![Exam page](/En/Images-New/uLMS/take-exams/5.png)

### Save answers and submit the exam

During the exam, the system automatically saves answers at regular intervals. Students can also click **[Save answers]** to save them manually.

After answering the questions, click **[Submit exam]** to submit the exam paper and exit the exam. If any questions remain unanswered, the system displays a prompt when the exam is submitted.

![Save answers and submit the exam](/En/Images-New/uLMS/take-exams/6.png)

### View your score

After the exam ends, students can view their exam scores in the **[Exams]** section.

![View exam scores](/En/Images-New/uLMS/take-exams/7.png)
