# Participate in classroom interactions

## 1. Navigation path

**[Course details]–[Classroom]**

![Open the course Classroom section](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/1.png)

## 2. Steps

### View the class-session list

In the **[Classroom]** list, students can filter and access class sessions by status and class time. They can also view classroom activities. Use the options at the top of the page to switch between class sessions and activities.

Class-session statuses and available actions are as follows:

- **Not started**: The class session cannot be entered yet.
- **In progress**: Click **[Enter class]** to attend the class.
- **Ended**: Click **[Classroom report]** to view related information.

![View the class-session list and statuses](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/2.png)

### Enter a class session

Click **[Enter class]** to open the main classroom page. The page contains an area for activities started by the teacher and an area containing classroom activity records.

![Enter the main classroom page](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/3.png)

### Participate in classroom interactions

#### 1. Participate in roll call

After the teacher starts **[Roll call]**, the roll-call window automatically appears on the student's classroom page. Students must check in using the method selected by the teacher. The platform currently supports three methods: numeric code check-in, QR code check-in, and one-tap check-in. QR code check-in is not supported on a computer. The uLearning app supports all three methods.

- **Numeric code check-in**: Within the valid period set by the teacher, enter the numeric code displayed on the teacher's screen and click **[Confirm]** to submit it.

![Check in using a numeric code](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/4.png)

- **One-tap check-in**: Within the valid period set by the teacher, click **[Check in]**.

![Use one-tap check-in](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/5.png)

#### 2. View student selection

After the teacher starts **[Student selection]**, view the interaction results through **[Student selection]** in the activity-list area.

![View classroom student-selection results](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/6.png)

If you are selected, the system displays a corresponding notification.

![View a notification that you were selected](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/7.png)

#### 3. Participate in Buzzer

After the teacher starts **[Buzzer]**, a question window automatically appears on the student's classroom page. Click **[Buzzer]** to participate.

![Participate in classroom Buzzer](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/8.png)

After you successfully buzz in, the dialog status displays "Buzzed in," and all students who successfully buzzed in are shown at the bottom.

![View successful Buzzer results](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/9.png)

#### 4. Participate in a poll

After the teacher starts **[Poll]**, the poll window automatically appears on the student's classroom page. Select an option based on the poll content and submit your response to participate.

![Participate in a classroom poll](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/10.png)

#### 5. Participate in a quiz

After the teacher starts **[Quiz]**, the quiz window automatically appears on the student's classroom page. Complete the quiz and click **[Submit]**.

![Participate in a classroom quiz](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/11.png)

#### 6. Answer a question

After the teacher starts **[Question]**, the question window automatically appears on the student's classroom page. Answer the question and click **[Submit]**.

![Answer a classroom question](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/12.png)

#### 7. Participate in a discussion

After the teacher starts **[Discussion]**, the discussion window automatically appears on the student's classroom page. Complete your response and click **[Submit]**.

![Participate in a classroom discussion](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/13.png)

#### 8. Participate in photo upload

After the teacher starts **[Photo upload]**, the photo-upload window automatically appears on the student's classroom page. Complete the activity and click **[Submit]**.

![Participate in photo upload](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/14.png)

#### 9. Join a live session

After the teacher starts **[Live session]**, the **[Teacher is live]** button appears on the student's classroom page. Click it to enter the live session.

![Enter a classroom live session](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/15.png)

#### 10. View classroom resources

When the teacher displays class resources using **[Screen sharing]**, students can view them at the same time.

![View resources shared on the teacher's screen](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/16.png)

After the teacher distributes a resource using **[Send to students]** under **[Resources]**, students can click **[View]** for the corresponding document to open it.

![View a classroom resource sent by the teacher](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/17.png)

![Open classroom resource content](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/18.png)

### View the classroom report

Students can use **[Classroom report]** to view their classroom performance, including total points, class ranking, and detailed scores for classroom interactions.

![Open the personal classroom report](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/19.png)

![View personal classroom performance](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/20.png)

### View classroom activities

Students can view classroom activities through **[Activities]**.

![View classroom activities](/En/Images-New/uLMS/participate-in-classroom-interactions/21.png)
