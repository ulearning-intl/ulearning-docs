# Manage announcements

## 1. Navigation path

**[Course details]–[Announcements]**

![Open course announcements](/En/Images-New/uLMS/announcement-management/1.png)

## 2. Steps

### Create an announcement

Use **[Announcements]** to publish course-related notices and announcements. Course administrators can publish to all classes. Teachers and teaching assistants can publish only to classes they teach.

Enter the announcement content, select the classes and publication time, and publish the announcement.

![Create a course announcement](/En/Images-New/uLMS/announcement-management/2.png)

![Enter announcement content and publication scope](/En/Images-New/uLMS/announcement-management/3.png)

### Edit an announcement

Click **[...]–[Edit]** to edit an announcement.

![Edit an announcement](/En/Images-New/uLMS/announcement-management/4.png)

### View announcements

#### 1. View announcement details

The announcement list shows partial information. Use **[Expand/Collapse]** to show or hide the full content.

![Expand or collapse announcement content](/En/Images-New/uLMS/announcement-management/5.png)

#### 2. View the number of readers

Click **[Number read/Total recipients in classes]** to view details of student announcement reading.

![View announcement reading details](/En/Images-New/uLMS/announcement-management/6.png)

### Export announcements

Set the export format and time range, then export. Excel and PDF formats are supported.

![Set announcement export format and time range](/En/Images-New/uLMS/announcement-management/7.png)

### Delete an announcement

Click **[...]–[Delete]** to delete an announcement. Deleted content cannot be recovered.

![Delete an announcement](/En/Images-New/uLMS/announcement-management/8.png)
