# Manage courses

## 1. Navigation path

**[Course list]–[Manage courses]**

![Open course management from the course list](/En/Images-New/uLMS/course-management/1.png)

## 2. Steps

### Create a course

Click **[Create course]** and enter the course information in the dialog: "Course name" and "Course cover." "Course category" is optional.

After entering the course information, click **[Create]** to create a new course.

![Enter information and create a course](/En/Images-New/uLMS/course-management/2.png)

### Edit a course

Open the course through **[Course list]–[Edit course]**, or edit its information through **[Course details]–[Settings]–[Course information]**.

![Edit a course from the course list](/En/Images-New/uLMS/course-management/3.png)

![Edit course information in course settings](/En/Images-New/uLMS/course-management/4.png)

### Copy a course

Copy a course through **[Course list]–[More]–[Offer course again]**. You can use the current course as a template and copy its content and activities to a new offering. Copied activities are initially in the "Unpublished" state.

![Select Offer course again](/En/Images-New/uLMS/course-management/5.png)

![Copy course content and activities](/En/Images-New/uLMS/course-management/6.png)

### End a course

End a course through **[Course list]–[More]–[End course]**. After a course ends, teachers cannot publish new resources or activities or edit existing course content; they can only view and export data. Students can continue browsing course content but cannot join the course.

![End a course](/En/Images-New/uLMS/course-management/7.png)

### Delete a course

Delete a course through **[Course list]–[More]–[Delete course]**. A deleted course cannot be recovered, and students who joined it can no longer continue learning.

![Delete a course](/En/Images-New/uLMS/course-management/8.png)
