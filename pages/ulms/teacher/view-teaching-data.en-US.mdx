## 2. Steps

### AI analysis

#### 1. Learning analytics

- **Add charts**: Select analysis dimensions under **[Add charts]** as needed and add charts to the analysis report.
- **Change a name**: Click the relevant entry to change a chart name.
- **Remove charts**: Remove added charts from the report.

![Edit learning-analytics charts](/En/Images-New/uLMS/view-teaching-data/8.png)

![View the learning-analytics report](/En/Images-New/uLMS/view-teaching-data/9.png)

#### 2. Student profiles

Use **[View all students]** to view the student list, then click a student to open their profile details.

![View all student profiles](/En/Images-New/uLMS/view-teaching-data/10.png)

Student profiles analyze classroom performance, AI analysis, learning tasks, classroom performance, knowledge mastery, assessment scores, AI-assistant usage, and other dimensions.

![View student-profile details](/En/Images-New/uLMS/view-teaching-data/11.png)
