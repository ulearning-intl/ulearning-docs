# LMS quick start for students

## Step 1: Log in to LMS

### Open the website

Select the domain for your site. The sites are:

1. Asia site (asia): [https://www.ulearning.asia/ulearning/index.html#/i18n](https://www.ulearning.asia/ulearning/index.html#/i18n)
2. Africa site (net): [https://www.ulearning.net/ulearning/index.html#/i18n](https://www.ulearning.net/ulearning/index.html#/i18n)
3. Latin America site (pro): [https://www.ulearning.pro/ulearning/index.html#/i18n](https://www.ulearning.pro/ulearning/index.html#/i18n)

![Choose an LMS site](/En/Images-New/uLMS/common/1.png)

### Register/log in

If you do not have an account, register first. If you have an account, enter your account information to log in (the website currently does not support registration; contact your administrator).

![Register or log in to LMS](/En/Images-New/uLMS/quick-start-for-students/2.png)

## Step 2: Join a course

Students must join the course before attending class. There are two ways:

- **Method 1**: The teacher links students to the course. Students do not need to join and can start learning directly.
- **Method 2**: Students join the course themselves by entering the course code.

![Join a course using the course code](/En/Images-New/uLMS/quick-start-for-students/3.png)

## Step 3: Study resources

- In **[Unit]**, review the course syllabus and study according to the teaching plan.
- In **[Courseware]**, study the courseware according to the learning plan.
- In **[Resources]**, view resources about this course uploaded by the teacher.

![Study course resources](/En/Images-New/uLMS/quick-start-for-students/4.png)

## Step 4: Attend class

- **In-person class**: Go to **[Classroom]–[Enter class]** to attend class.
- **Live class**: Find the corresponding live session in the live-session list and join it.

![Enter a classroom or live class](/En/Images-New/uLMS/quick-start-for-students/5.png)
