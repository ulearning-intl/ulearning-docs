# Log in and recover your password

## 1. Navigation path

Open the uClass client.

## 2. Steps

### 1. Log in to your account

#### (1) Open the login page

Select **[Log in]** to open the login page.

![Open the login page](/En/Images-New/uClass/login-and-password-recovery/1.png)

#### (2) Select a login method

Login methods include account-and-password login, Google login, and QR-code login.

![Log in with an account and password or Google](/En/Images-New/uClass/login-and-password-recovery/2.png)

![Log in with a QR code](/En/Images-New/uClass/login-and-password-recovery/3.png)

### 2. Recover your password

#### (1) Open the password-recovery page

Select **[Forgot your password?]** to go to the uLearning website and recover your password.

![Open the password-recovery page](/En/Images-New/uClass/login-and-password-recovery/4.png)

#### (2) Reset your password

Enter your email address and send the password-reset email. Follow the instructions in the email to reset your password.

![Reset your password by email](/En/Images-New/uClass/login-and-password-recovery/5.png)
