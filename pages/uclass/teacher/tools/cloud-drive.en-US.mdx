# Cloud drive

## 1. Navigation path

Open the right-side menu in uClass.

## 2. Steps

### 1. Open the cloud drive

Select **[Cloud drive]** in your personal space.

![Open the cloud drive in your personal space](/En/Images-New/uClass/cloud-drive/1.png)

### 2. Use cloud-drive resources

You can open or send resources directly from the resource library, classroom cloud drive, desktop, and local disks to students.

![Open or send cloud-drive resources](/En/Images-New/uClass/cloud-drive/2.png)
