# Screen sharing

## 1. Navigation path

Open the right-side menu in uClass.

## 2. Steps

### 1. Open screen sharing

Select **[Screen sharing]** in your personal space.

![Open screen sharing in your personal space](/En/Images-New/uClass/screen-casting/1.png)

### 2. Select the screen-sharing system

Select the relevant operating system, and then follow the instructions on the page to complete screen sharing.

![Select a system and complete screen sharing](/En/Images-New/uClass/screen-casting/2.jpg)
