# Recording

## 1. Navigation path

Open uClass and select **[Personal space]** in the left menu.

## 2. Steps

### 1. Open recordings

Select **[Recordings]** in your personal space.

![Open recordings in your personal space](/En/Images-New/uClass/recording/1.png)

### 2. Start recording

Set the video name and microphone, and then select **[Start recording]**.

![Set recording information and start recording](/En/Images-New/uClass/recording/2.png)

### 3. Pause or end recording

In the right toolbar, you can choose to pause or end the recording.

![Pause or end recording](/En/Images-New/uClass/recording/3.png)

### 4. Find the recording

After ending the recording, go to **[Cloud drive]** in your personal space to find the recording.

![Open the cloud drive in your personal space](/En/Images-New/uClass/recording/4.png)

### 5. Open classroom recordings

Select **[Classroom cloud drive]** -> **[Classroom recordings]** to find the recording.

![Find recordings in the classroom cloud drive](/En/Images-New/uClass/recording/5.png)

### 6. Open or send the recording

You can open the recording directly in the player or send it to students.

![Open or send the recording](/En/Images-New/uClass/recording/6.png)

### 7. Share with students in a class

Select a course and class session, and then select **[Send to students]** to share the video with all students in the selected class session.

![Share the recording with students](/En/Images-New/uClass/recording/7.png)
