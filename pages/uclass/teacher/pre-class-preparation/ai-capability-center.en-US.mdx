# AI capability center

## 1. Navigation path

**[AI capability center]**

![Open the AI capability center](/En/Images-New/uClass/ai-capability-center/1.png)

## 2. Steps

### 1. Use AI assistants

Use personal agents and favorite agents directly, or go to the agent marketplace to use agents published by your school.

![View available AI assistants](/En/Images-New/uClass/ai-capability-center/2.png)

Select an agent to open its details and use it directly.

### 2. Use AI tools

The platform provides several AI tools that teachers can call with one click as needed.

![View available AI tools](/En/Images-New/uClass/ai-capability-center/3.png)

#### (1) AI lesson preparation

The AI lesson-preparation tool helps teachers generate various teaching-material documents. Generated documents can be edited online, and teachers can use the AI lesson-preparation assistant to rewrite, expand, and optimize them. Completed teaching-material documents can be downloaded, saved to the resource library, or published to a class course with one click.

![Use AI lesson preparation](/En/Images-New/uClass/ai-capability-center/4.png)

#### (2) Lesson-plan generation

The lesson-plan generation tool intelligently matches the education stage and subject to a course-unit topic and uses reference materials such as uploaded course outlines to generate a complete lesson-plan document with one click. Generated lesson plans can also be edited online and downloaded.

![Use lesson-plan generation](/En/Images-New/uClass/ai-capability-center/5.png)

#### (3) Resource search

The resource-search tool searches internal and external resources that teachers can use as supplementary resources during teaching.

![Use resource search](/En/Images-New/uClass/ai-capability-center/6.png)

#### (4) AI grading

For open-ended questions in assignments and exams, the platform provides an AI grading tool that can score responses based on custom grading rules and generate feedback. The platform provides several grading-rule templates for assignments in different subjects. AI can also automatically generate a matching grading rule based on the question information.

![Use AI grading](/En/Images-New/uClass/ai-capability-center/7.png)

#### (5) PPT generation

The PPT-generation tool automatically creates a PPT with a three-level outline based on a topic or outline. The platform provides a wide range of PPT templates.

![Use PPT generation](/En/Images-New/uClass/ai-capability-center/8.png)

#### (6) AI question generation

The AI question-generation tool intelligently generates quiz questions based on knowledge points. Knowledge points can be entered manually or selected from an existing knowledge graph. Supported question types include multiple choice, multiple response, true/false, fill-in-the-blank, and short answer. Generated questions can be saved to the question bank for use in quizzes and exams.

![Use AI question generation](/En/Images-New/uClass/ai-capability-center/9.png)

#### (7) Video analysis

The video-analysis tool accepts video or audio uploads and intelligently generates summaries, captions, and word clouds to help students quickly understand teaching-video content.

![Use video analysis](/En/Images-New/uClass/ai-capability-center/10.png)

#### (8) Document reading

The document-reading tool supports AI-powered questions and answers and summary generation based on documents, helping students quickly read and understand teaching materials.

![Use document reading](/En/Images-New/uClass/ai-capability-center/11.png)
