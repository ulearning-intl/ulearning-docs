# AI feature settings

## 1. Navigation path

**[More]** -> **[Settings]** -> **[AI features]**

## 2. Steps

### 1. Open settings

Open the right-side menu, select **[More]**, and then select **[Settings]**.

![Open the right-side menu and select More](/En/Images-New/uClass/ai-feature-settings/1.png)

![Open settings](/En/Images-New/uClass/ai-feature-settings/2.png)

### 2. Turn on AI features

To turn on AI features, select the relevant options. If activation fails, contact your institution's administrator to configure the relevant services.

![Turn on AI features](/En/Images-New/uClass/ai-feature-settings/3.png)
