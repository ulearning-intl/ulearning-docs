## Steps

### Knowledge-base overview

The data-analytics page shows the total number of knowledge bases, resources, and question-answer pairs in the institution.

![View the knowledge-base overview](/En/Images-New/uAgent/knowledge-base-statistics/2.png)

### Knowledge-base contribution ranking

Select **[Start date]** and **[End date]**, and choose the relevant dates to view the knowledge-base contribution ranking for the selected period.

![View the knowledge-base contribution ranking](/En/Images-New/uAgent/knowledge-base-statistics/3.png)
