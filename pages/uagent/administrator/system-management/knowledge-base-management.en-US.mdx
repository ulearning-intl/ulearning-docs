# Knowledge-base management

## Navigation path

**[System management]** - **[Knowledge base]**

![Knowledge-base management navigation path](/En/Images-New/uAgent/knowledge-base-management/1.png)

## Steps

### View knowledge bases

1. Find a knowledge base directly in the list or search for it in the list.

   ![Search for a knowledge base](/En/Images-New/uAgent/knowledge-base-management/2.png)
2. After finding the knowledge base whose details you want to view, select **[View knowledge base]** at the far right of the corresponding row to open its subpage.

   ![Open the knowledge-base subpage](/En/Images-New/uAgent/knowledge-base-management/3.png)
3. Select **[Resources]** to view all resources uploaded to the knowledge base. Select a filename to preview or download the resource.

   ![View knowledge-base resources](/En/Images-New/uAgent/knowledge-base-management/4.png)

   ![Preview or download a knowledge-base resource](/En/Images-New/uAgent/knowledge-base-management/5.png)
4. Select **[Question-answer pairs]** to view all question-answer pairs created in the knowledge base.

   ![View knowledge-base question-answer pairs](/En/Images-New/uAgent/knowledge-base-management/6.png)

### Knowledge-base statistics

1. Select **[View knowledge-base statistics]** to open the data-analytics page. The page shows the total number of knowledge bases, resources, and question-answer pairs in the institution, resource distribution, and knowledge-base contribution ranking.

   ![Open the knowledge-base statistics page](/En/Images-New/uAgent/knowledge-base-management/7.png)

   ![View knowledge-base statistics](/En/Images-New/uAgent/knowledge-base-management/8.png)
2. Select **[Start date]** and **[End date]**, and choose the relevant dates to view the knowledge-base contribution ranking for the selected period.

   ![Search the knowledge-base contribution ranking](/En/Images-New/uAgent/knowledge-base-management/9.png)
