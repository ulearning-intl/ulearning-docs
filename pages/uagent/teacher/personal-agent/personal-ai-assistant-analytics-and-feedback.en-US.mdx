# Personal AI assistant analytics and feedback

## 1. Navigation path

**[Personal space]** -> **[AI assistants]** -> **[Analytics]** or **[Feedback]**

![Open AI assistant analytics or feedback](/En/Images-New/uAgent/personal-ai-assistant-analytics-and-feedback/1.png)

## 2. Steps

### 1. Data analytics

1. Select **[Analytics]**.

   ![Open data analytics](/En/Images-New/uAgent/personal-ai-assistant-analytics-and-feedback/2.png)
2. On the **[Analytics]** page, view AI-assistant visits, visit trends, and conversation details. You can also export the details.

   ![View AI-assistant analytics](/En/Images-New/uAgent/personal-ai-assistant-analytics-and-feedback/3.png)

### 2. Feedback

1. Select **[Feedback]**.

   ![Open the feedback page](/En/Images-New/uAgent/personal-ai-assistant-analytics-and-feedback/4.png)
2. On the **[Feedback]** page, view feedback submitted by users about the AI assistant.

   ![View AI-assistant feedback](/En/Images-New/uAgent/personal-ai-assistant-analytics-and-feedback/5.png)
