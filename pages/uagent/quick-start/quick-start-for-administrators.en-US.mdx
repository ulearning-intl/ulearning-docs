# uAgent quick start for administrators

## Step 1: Log in to the AI administration system

### 1. Open the website

Select the domain for your site:

1. Asia site: [https://ai.ulearning.asia/](https://ai.ulearning.asia/)
2. Africa site: [https://ai.ulearning.net/](https://ai.ulearning.net/)
3. Latin America site: [https://ai.ulearning.pro/](https://ai.ulearning.pro/)

![Open the AI administration system for the relevant site](/En/Images-New/uAgent/quick-start-for-administrators/1.png)

### 2. Enter your account and log in

Enter the administrator account information and log in to the AI administration system.

![Log in to the AI administration system](/En/Images-New/uAgent/quick-start-for-administrators/2.png)

## Step 2: Manage AI agents

### 1. Manage AI assistants

Create a personal AI assistant or team AI assistant, and orchestrate it after creation.

![Create and manage AI assistants](/En/Images-New/uAgent/quick-start-for-administrators/3.png)

Set roles, associate instruction sets, knowledge bases, and skills, and configure sharing permissions.

![Orchestrate an AI assistant](/En/Images-New/uAgent/quick-start-for-administrators/4.png)

### 2. Knowledge bases

Train agents through knowledge bases and question-answer pairs.

![Train an agent through knowledge bases and question-answer pairs](/En/Images-New/uAgent/quick-start-for-administrators/5.png)

### 3. Workflows

A workflow is a tool for automating task processing through visual workflow orchestration. A workflow can be published as a standalone application or used as an agent plug-in that is called in a conversation through language instructions.

![Create and orchestrate a workflow](/En/Images-New/uAgent/quick-start-for-administrators/6.png)

### 4. Instruction sets

Create instruction templates to provide agents with quick prompts.

![Create an instruction set](/En/Images-New/uAgent/quick-start-for-administrators/7.png)

## Step 3: Manage the team space

After creating a team agent, manage team members in **[Members and settings]**. Other operations are the same as those in the personal space.

![Manage team-space members](/En/Images-New/uAgent/quick-start-for-administrators/8.png)

## Step 4: Data analytics

View model-usage and knowledge-base data analytics.

![View data analytics](/En/Images-New/uAgent/quick-start-for-administrators/9.png)

## Step 5: System management

Manage users, agents, knowledge bases, applications, and websites.

![Perform system management](/En/Images-New/uAgent/quick-start-for-administrators/10.png)
